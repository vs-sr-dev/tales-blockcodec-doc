The same character models, packed twice.

Nineteen files under the same names appear on the 2003 GameCube discs and
inside the 2004 PlayStation 2 build's TOSROOT volume.  For every one of them
the codec census reports the *same number of blocks* and the *same total
unpacked length* -- so the packer was handed identical input and segmented it
the same way both times -- and a different packed length.

That is the only direct measurement of the packer this corpus has.  Section 8
of the specification says the tool itself has left no trace beyond its output;
here its output was produced twice from one input, a year apart.
