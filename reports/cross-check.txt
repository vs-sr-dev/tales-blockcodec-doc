   20085 blocks compared, 20085 byte-identical, 0 differing

   verify.py carries a second ring machine of its own, written to force a
   wrong dictionary for the negative control.  Running it with the correct
   dictionary makes it an independent decoder, and it agrees with
   tales_block.py over the whole Tales of Eternia corpus.

The two title pipelines were written independently: the Super Famicom one
decodes against the output buffer, the PlayStation one against a ring.
tales_block.py implements one ring machine for both and reproduces each.
